Toondio: Privacy Policy

Updated November 16, 2026.

Toondio is a Windows app for making animated 3D videos, provided by LATA. Projects and media are saved on your computer. Optional AI requests send text and story context to the AI provider you choose, and optional ElevenLabs voices send the lines they speak to ElevenLabs. If you connect your own AI app (Claude, Codex or ChatGPT on your computer), the stories you choose to Send are shared with that app on your computer. Toondio has no user account system, advertising or analytics.

Local storage

Stories, recordings, imported media, chats and settings are stored locally. Exported files are saved where you choose. The optional AI assistant and ElevenLabs voices described below are the exceptions to local processing.

Toondio also keeps a copy of each story as files in its data folder (the stories folder), so a story can be brought back if the app's browser storage is ever cleared.

For an unpackaged installation, app data is under %LOCALAPPDATA%\Toondio\Data. For a Store installation, it is under %LOCALAPPDATA%\Packages\<package-family>\LocalCache\Local\Toondio\Data. Help → Settings & storage → Open data folder shows the actual location. Browser storage in that folder contains projects and chats. Saved API keys are encrypted with Windows data protection, tied to your Windows account; other local content is not separately encrypted by Toondio.

When migrating an older CLARIO or portable profile, Toondio copies it and retains the old folder for recovery. That old folder can contain projects, recordings, chats and encrypted keys too.

Microphone, camera and spelling

The microphone is used to record a line on your request. Recordings are stored locally and are not sent to AI services or to ElevenLabs. Windows privacy settings control microphone access.

The camera is used only when you turn it on in Characters › Me in the story, to film yourself into a story. Cutting you out of your video or camera picture, with or without a green screen, runs on your computer; the picture is not uploaded. Windows privacy settings control camera access.

Spelling uses the Windows spell checker; Add to dictionary changes your Windows user dictionary.

Optional downloads

Some features download free components the first time you turn them on. Each download is checked against a fixed fingerprint before use and kept in the app's data folder. The site serving it (GitHub, the Python Package Index or Hugging Face) receives ordinary network information, including your IP address.

  • Real voices (Kokoro): about 137–344 MB in total: a Kokoro model from the sherpa-onnx GitHub releases and Toondio's separate voice engine from its GitHub releases. After installation, Kokoro speech is generated locally; your dialogue text is not sent anywhere for Kokoro speech.
  • Voice maker: a private Python runtime and its packages (the uv installer from GitHub, Python packages from the Python Package Index) and a voice model from Hugging Face. Making a voice from your recording runs on your computer.
  • Exact lip sync: Rhubarb Lip Sync, about 84 MB, from its GitHub releases. It listens to your voice takes on your computer; nothing is uploaded.

Help → Settings & storage can unload or delete the downloaded voices. Already saved takes remain in stories.

Optional ElevenLabs voices

If you connect your own ElevenLabs account with your ElevenLabs API key and give a character an ElevenLabs voice, the text of that character's lines is sent to ElevenLabs to be spoken, with the chosen voice and its settings. ElevenLabs returns the audio, which is saved in your story. Toondio also asks ElevenLabs for your list of voices and your remaining credits. Speaking lines uses your ElevenLabs credits. Recordings, videos and other story content are not sent.

ElevenLabs' own terms, privacy policy, retention and charges apply. Your key is stored encrypted as described under Local storage and is not included in project exports. Removing it in Connections deletes the saved local key; revoke it on the ElevenLabs website to invalidate it.

Optional AI assistant

You choose a provider and supply an API key. Supported providers are Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude, OpenAI, Groq and OpenRouter. A connected provider receives your key for authentication. Saved keys are not returned to the page or included in project exports.

When you send a chat, Toondio sends your message, recent conversation, current script, character and scene details, and relevant earlier episode scripts directly to the selected provider. Recording and imported media bytes are not attached. Text you type or include in story context is sent, so avoid including private information you do not want that provider to receive.

The provider's terms, privacy rules, retention, age limits and charges apply. OpenRouter may route requests to another model provider. Check those policies before connecting. Toondio does not operate a server that receives these requests. Removing a key in Connections deletes the saved local key; revoke it with the provider to invalidate it. Deleting local chats does not delete information already sent to a provider.

Your AI app (optional)

You can let an AI app on your computer (Claude, Codex or ChatGPT) make videos with Toondio.

  • Connect: when you press Connect in AI › Your AI app, Toondio adds its helper to that app's own settings and keeps a backup of them. Nothing changes until you press Connect.
  • The helper: it talks only to Toondio on the same computer, through a local connection with a key that changes every session. Toondio does the work in its own window.
  • What the app receives: only the stories you Send in AI › Your AI app (characters, looks, voices, your idea and, if you choose, your lines). They are saved as files in Documents › Toondio Maker › Kits, which the app reads. Remove one there, or in Shared, to stop sharing it.
  • The app's own terms apply: your AI app may send what it reads to its own company (for example Anthropic or OpenAI), under that app's terms and privacy policy. Toondio does not operate a server that receives any of it.
  • ElevenLabs credits: an AI app cannot spend them by itself. Toondio asks you in its own window first, unless you set a limit for it to spend without asking.

Logs and links

The app keeps toondio.log in its data folder and replaces it on startup. It records technical events and errors and is not automatically uploaded. Review it before voluntarily sharing it with support. Provider error bodies are excluded from persistent request-failure logs.

Links you choose open in your browser. Those sites apply their own privacy policies.

Children

Toondio does not knowingly collect children's personal information. The optional AI providers have their own eligibility and age requirements. A parent, guardian or teacher should review those requirements and what a child may send before enabling AI.

Removing data

Back up exported projects and any source videos before removing app data. Windows normally removes package-private data when a Store app is uninstalled or reset; that can include unsaved-to-file stories. Exported files, original imported files and retained migration copies outside the package are separate and remain until you delete them. For a portable installation, close the app before deleting its data folder. Use Open data folder to identify the correct location rather than guessing it. Keys can also be revoked at the provider's website.
